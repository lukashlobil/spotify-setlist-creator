Privacy Policy

Extension: Setlist.fm to Spotify • Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

Setlist.fm to Spotify is a browser extension that reads concert setlist information from setlist.fm pages you visit and lets you create matching playlists directly in your Spotify account. The extension operates entirely within your browser and does not use any developer-owned backend server.

2. Data Processed and Stored Locally

The extension stores the following data strictly locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local:

  • Spotify Client ID: The Client ID you enter to authenticate with Spotify.
  • Spotify OAuth Tokens: Access and refresh tokens obtained via Spotify OAuth 2.0 PKCE so you remain signed in across sessions.
  • Basic Spotify Profile Info: Your Spotify user ID, display name, and avatar URL, used solely to show which account is connected.
  • Recent Playlist History: Names, URLs, and track counts of playlists you created through the extension (up to 15 entries).

You can delete your stored Spotify tokens and profile information at any time by clicking Sign out in the extension, or delete all stored data by uninstalling the extension.

3. External Network Requests

The extension communicates exclusively with official Spotify endpoints when you initiate authentication, track matching, or playlist creation:

  • https://accounts.spotify.com (OAuth 2.0 PKCE authorization and token refresh)
  • https://api.spotify.com (searching tracks, reading your basic profile, and creating playlists in your library)

When you visit a setlist.fm/setlist/* page, the extension reads the visible concert artist, venue, date, and song titles from the page DOM locally in your browser.

4. No Tracking, Analytics, or Data Sharing

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with the extension developer or third parties (other than your direct requests to Spotify's API).
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or remote code execution.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project's repository or contact the developer via the support email listed on the Chrome Web Store page.
